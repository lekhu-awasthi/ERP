Used for a Product with no Sales Account of its own.

Debited on Invoice approval, credited on Payment approval.

Only needed for invoices/credit notes with VAT-rated lines.

Purchase-Side Defaults

Used for a Service product with no Purchase Account of its own. Goods products always use the Inventory Account below instead, regardless of any Purchase Account set on the product.

Credited on Purchase Bill/Expense approval, debited on Supplier Payment approval.

Input-tax-credit account for VAT-rated purchases -- distinct from the Sales-side VAT Payable account above.

Only needed for purchase bills/expenses with TDS applied.

Inventory-Side Defaults

The stock asset account -- credited on Invoice/InventoryAdjustment-decrease approval, debited on PurchaseBill/InventoryAdjustment-increase approval.

Only needed for invoicing Goods products -- debited for the FIFO-computed cost of what's sold.

The variance/contra account Inventory Adjustments post against -- required before approving one.
