Migration

Bring your previous system's statutory Sales and Purchase register history across at cutover. Runs in the background — you can leave this page.

Configurations
Migrated rows are statutory report data only. They appear in the two Migrated Register reports and nowhere else. They are never posted to the General Ledger, never become Invoices or Purchase Bills, produce no stock movements or payments, and do not affect the Trial Balance, VAT Summary, Annex 5, Annex 13 or the TDS report. This is import only — there is no way to edit or export migrated rows back out from here, and a document number that has already been imported is rejected rather than duplicated.
